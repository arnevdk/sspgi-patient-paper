\section{Questionnaire for self-reported eye motor assessment}
\label{app:oculomotor-questions}

In addition to the visual acuity test and the details obtained from the medical
file or the oculomotor assessment provided by the clinician, the following questions
were asked to poll for self-reported eye movement and vision difficulties.
\begin{enumerate}
	\item Do you experience any fatigue or discomfort when moving the eyes?
	\item Do you experience any fatigue or discomfort when fixating the gaze?
	\item Do you experience any involuntary eye movements, including tremors
	      jitters or larger movements or drifts?
	\item Do you experience discomfort or the inability to perform  eye movements
	      in specific directions?
	\item Are there any parts of the field of view that you cannot or cannot
	      comfortably view, redirect the eyes to or gaze at?
	\item Do you experience any other issues with vision?
	\item Do you experience any other issues with eye movement?
\end{enumerate}
