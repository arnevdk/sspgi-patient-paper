\section{Questionnaire for basic clinical evaluation}
\label{app:clinical-questions}

In addition to the information provided by the paramedical team, the supervising clinician
and the information obtained through observation by the experimenters, the following
information was gathered on a structured basis:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Age
\item Handedness
\item Sex
\item Neuromuscular diagnosis
\item Time of neuromuscular diagnosis if applicable
\item Stage of the neuromuscular disease if applicable
\item Ophtalmological diagnosis
\item Time of ophtalmolocical diagnosis if applicable
\end{enumerate}
